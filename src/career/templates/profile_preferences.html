Salary and distance only exclude a listing when the source actually states a salary, or the listing's location is one of a small list of UK towns/cities this app recognises — most aren't, and an unrecognised one is never excluded on that account. A location naming a country or major city outside the UK (Germany, Berlin, Zürich, Paris…) counts as too far, but a small foreign town the app doesn't know still gets through. Required keywords are checked against the listing's title and description as the source provides it — Reed only gives a short snippet, so a role that mentions Azure further down its full advert can still be excluded.
